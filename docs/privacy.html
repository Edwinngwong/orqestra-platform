Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the Orqestra development service ("Orqestra," "we," or "us") handles information when people use its human-governed software-delivery workflows, including the WhatsApp Muse intake channel.

Information we process

  • Account and workspace information, including names, email addresses, roles, and authentication records.
  • WhatsApp identifiers such as the sender number, business phone-number ID, message ID, and the content of messages explicitly addressed to @muse.
  • Project and delivery information, including repository identifiers, issue or Jira references, proposals, approval decisions, test results, pull-request references, and audit events.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, route authorized requests, interpret requests into reviewable proposals, execute only human-approved software-delivery steps, communicate status, prevent replay or abuse, maintain audit records, and improve reliability and security.

Service providers and integrations

Depending on the customer configuration, information may be processed by Meta and WhatsApp for messaging, OpenAI for request interpretation or approved coding workflows, GitHub for repositories and issues, Atlassian Jira for work tracking, and infrastructure providers used to host the service. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information or use WhatsApp message content for advertising. We disclose information only to operate configured integrations, comply with law, protect rights and security, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention

We retain information while it is needed to provide the service, maintain security and auditability, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Development and diagnostic data may be retained until it is manually removed. Customers should avoid sending secrets or unnecessary personal information through intake channels.

Security

Orqestra uses access controls, signed webhook verification, scoped integrations, audit records, isolated execution, and human approvals. No system is completely secure, and users remain responsible for protecting their accounts and integration credentials.

Your choices and deletion

You may request access, correction, or deletion by following our data-deletion instructions. We may retain limited information where required for security, legal compliance, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

International processing and children

Information may be processed where our service providers operate. Orqestra is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service evolves and will revise the effective date. Questions may be sent to edwinng95@hotmail.com.
