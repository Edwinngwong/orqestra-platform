Data Deletion

Instructions for Orqestra users

Request deletion

Email edwinng95@hotmail.com with the subject Orqestra data deletion request.

Include enough information to locate your records, such as your Orqestra account email, workspace name, and the WhatsApp number or identifier connected to the service. Do not send passwords, access tokens, private keys, or other secrets.

Verification and completion

We may ask you to verify control of the relevant account or phone number before deletion. After verification, we will remove or anonymize applicable account, WhatsApp intake, proposal, and workspace records within 30 days, unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

Third-party records

Deleting Orqestra data does not automatically delete issues, pull requests, messages, logs, or other records held by Meta, WhatsApp, GitHub, Atlassian, OpenAI, cloud providers, or customer-controlled systems. Contact those providers or the relevant workspace administrator for their deletion procedures.

Confirmation

We will confirm when the request is completed or explain any information that must be retained and why.
